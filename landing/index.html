Your family's DNA and health record, on your own device.

Hearth reads the raw DNA files you already downloaded, keeps a health log for everyone in the family, and charts what matters over time. All of it lives in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, because there is nowhere to upload it to.

Open the app View on GitHub

Works in any modern browser; install it like an app from the address bar. Your data stays where you opened it.

Private by design, not by promise

Stored only in your browser

DNA files, the family tree, health log, documents and notes are kept in your browser's private storage on this device. Clearing site data erases them; we could not restore them because we never had them.

No server, no account, no tracking

Hearth is a set of static files. There is no backend that could receive your data, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts. A test in the code fails if anything but the app's own files is fetched.

You decide what leaves

Backups are encrypted with your passphrase and written to a folder or drive you pick. Asking an AI is opt-in: you see every line of the context first, then copy it yourself or send it with your own API key.

What it does

Reads your raw DNA

AncestryDNA, 23andMe, MyHeritage, FamilyTreeDNA, Living DNA, Genotek, VCF and generic files, detected automatically. Import several family members at once.

A family, not just a genome

Parents and children in one pedigree, with Mendelian consistency checks and shared-genotype comparisons across relatives.

Findings with evidence

Your genotypes against a hand-reviewed knowledge base — pharmacogenomics, cardiometabolic and traits — with evidence grades and sources for every statement.

A health log for everyone

Symptoms with details, home measurements, lab results, diagnoses and medications. Read a lab report from text or a PDF on the device, and keep the original pages attached.

Charts that explain themselves

Every number over time, one line per person. Children's weight, height and BMI against the WHO growth curves; adults against standard ranges for BMI, blood pressure and pulse.

Ask, on your terms

Build a context pack from the findings and health log you choose, preview it, then paste it into an assistant you trust or send it with your own key.

Backups and sync

Encrypted snapshots to a folder synced by Google Drive, Dropbox or a USB stick; on a phone, through the share sheet. Load them on another computer to continue.

In your language

Twenty languages, light and dark themes, and a layout that works on a phone. Export open CSV and JSON files whenever you want your data elsewhere.

How it works

  1. Open the app. It loads once and then works offline. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for.
  2. Add your family. Import the raw DNA files from your testing company and start a health log.
  3. Keep a backup. Choose a folder or drive; Hearth writes an encrypted snapshot after every change.

Open source

The whole of Hearth — the app, the knowledge base and the phone apps — is public under the GNU AGPL-3.0. Anyone can read how it handles your data, check that it does what this page says, and run their own copy. Changes made by anyone who hosts it must stay open too.
