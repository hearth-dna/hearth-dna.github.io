Hearth

A family genome browser that keeps every file on your own device. No account. No upload. Export a dump when you want to move it.

  • Imports AncestryDNA, 23andMe, MyHeritage, FamilyTreeDNA, Living DNA, Genotek VCF and generic raw files.
  • Stores the whole family with a pedigree; checks Mendelian consistency between parents and children.
  • Reads your genotypes against a reviewed knowledge base with evidence grades and sources.
  • Builds a context pack you can paste into any chat assistant you already trust — after you have previewed every line.
