Privacy notice

Draft — review with a lawyer before public release.

In plain words

Hearth runs in your browser. Your DNA files, family tree, documents, notes and consent records are stored in your browser's private storage on your device. We operate no account system and no server that receives this data. If you clear site data without exporting a dump, it is gone; we cannot recover it because we never had it.

What our servers see

Hearth is static files on GitHub Pages: there is no backend of ours. Loading the pages and the public knowledge base sends GitHub your IP address and the usual browser headers, as any website does, and nothing about your genome. We run no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts.

What can leave your device, and only when you act

  • Backups. You pick a folder (a USB stick, or one that Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive syncs) or share a backup file yourself. Backups are encrypted with your passphrase unless you choose otherwise; whatever syncs the folder does so under its own terms. The phone apps can also sign in to Google Drive or Dropbox for you.
  • Copy-out context packs. You copy a text block and paste it into a third-party assistant yourself. The app shows you the exact text first and records what you copied in a local sharing log.
  • Your own API key. If you enter a provider key (to ask a question, or to read a photographed document with Google Gemini), the previewed content is sent directly from your browser to that provider under your contract with them. The key is stored only in your browser.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, requests for access, correction or deletion are satisfied by the app itself: Settings → Erase all data. For anything else, write to contact@hearth.example.

Relatives

A genome reveals information about biological relatives who did not choose to use this tool. Import only your own DNA or that of people who have explicitly agreed, and only a child's DNA if you are their parent or guardian.
