Privacy notice

Draft — review with a lawyer before public release.

In plain words

Hearth runs in your browser. Your DNA files, family tree, documents, notes and consent records are stored in your browser's private storage on your device. We operate no account system and no server that receives this data. If you clear site data without exporting a dump, it is gone; we cannot recover it because we never had it.

What our servers see

The static app files and the public knowledge base are served from a content delivery network. Those requests carry your IP address and the usual browser headers, as any website does, and nothing about your genome. We run no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts.

Optional features that send data

  • Copy-out context packs. You copy a text block and paste it into a third-party assistant yourself. That disclosure is your act and is governed by that provider's terms. The app shows you the exact text first and records what you copied in a local sharing log.
  • Your own API key. If you enter a provider key, the previewed pack is sent directly from your browser to that provider under your contract with them. The key is stored only in your browser.
  • Helper service. If enabled, the previewed pack is forwarded by our stateless helper service in the EU to the model provider. It is processed in memory, not logged, not stored, and not linked to any identifier. We ask for your explicit consent before the first use and you can withdraw it by switching the feature off.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, requests for access, correction or deletion are satisfied by the app itself: Settings → Erase all data. For anything else, write to contact@hearth.example.

Relatives

A genome reveals information about biological relatives who did not choose to use this tool. Import only your own DNA or that of people who have explicitly agreed, and only a child's DNA if you are their parent or guardian.
